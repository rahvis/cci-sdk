This is the mechanism the product names specifically for lending: coverage calibrated per channel, so a channel with weaker data can't hide behind a strong blended number. With only 20 calibration examples per channel here, per-channel numbers are imprecise — small-sample noise, not a flaw in the mechanism itself.

Where a hand-picked cutoff got it backwards

LT-0011 · online channel · true tier: decline

Unemployed applicant, requesting a limit larger than typical for the profile — every measure of this policy says decline.

Baseline:Correctly predicted "decline" — but only 75% self-reported confidence, just under its own 80% cutoff, so it sat in the queue anyway
CCI:Candidate set narrowed to a single tier ("decline"), Gate cleared its calibrated bound, auto-finalized

A calibrated bound doesn't just act less often on shaky evidence — it also acts confidently when the evidence is actually clear, instead of getting stuck on an arbitrary round number.

The actual guarantees returned, in plain terms

Tier check (Set, per channel): the calibrated candidate set contained the correct tier at least 80% of the time, audited separately within each of the 3 channels.
Auto-approval check (Gate): the expected rate of decisions that are auto-finalized and wrong is at most 15% — computed from held-out outcomes, not self-reported.
